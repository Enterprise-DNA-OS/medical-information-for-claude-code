# Why the desk works without a front end

The weekly work is assigning requests, inspecting evidence references, recording review outcomes and reconciling handoffs. A coordinator can do that through a coding assistant backed by the same database. Read-only HTML snapshots give the week, handoffs and evidence review without an application server.

A vendor screen provides mobile capture, permissioned collaboration, signed workflows and connected content delivery that this local base does not provide. HTML here is a snapshot, not a live portal. No offline sync, attachments viewer, electronic signatures or authenticated role management ships in the free base. The controlled medical-document system and pharmacovigilance system remain in place. Enterprise DNA scopes access controls, migration, screens and integrations around those existing responsibilities.

The database is the product. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor is the operator's door. A specialist remains responsible for clinical content.
